Division of marital loans and debts in Russia

Analytical adaptation of the Russian original; individual cases require separate assessment.

When a debt is shared

Under Articles 39(3) and 45 of the Russian Family Code, shared debts are allocated proportionately to awarded shares. A loan taken during marriage is not automatically shared: establish joint undertaking or use of all proceeds for family needs.

Evidence and burden of proof

The spouse seeking allocation ordinarily must prove the family purpose. Collect loan agreements, bank statements, purchase and repair invoices, correspondence and evidence of payments after separation. Bankruptcy proceedings may involve different evidentiary considerations.

Allocation and repayment

Shared debts may be allocated without simultaneous division of assets. A spouse who repaid a shared debt from personal funds after separation may seek an appropriate contribution, depending on the circumstances and shares.

Bank and mortgage rights

Internal allocation does not replace the contractual borrower or release them without creditor consent. Mortgage security survives division of the property. Consider involving the bank and assess the outstanding balance, payments and contract terms before filing.
