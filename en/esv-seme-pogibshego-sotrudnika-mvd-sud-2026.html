Housing benefit for a deceased Russian police officer’s family

Analytical adaptation; see the Russian original for the full case review and sample claim.

The five-year restriction

Under Article 4(7) of Federal Law No. 247-FZ, the restriction concerns intentional deterioration of housing conditions to qualify for registration. A sale within five years does not alone establish that intention.

Case reviewed by the Supreme Court

The officer transferred to Kamchatka in 2017. His family sold their former home in 2018. Following his death in 2022, his widow applied for registration for the one-time housing benefit. The commission refused on the basis of the sale. In determination No. 60-KG25-1-K9, the Supreme Court restored the first-instance decision after the appellate courts failed to assess the actual circumstances.

Evidence and eligibility

Obtain the transfer order, sale agreement, bank records, evidence of loan repayments, current housing arrangements, service and death records, and the written commission decision. Eligibility under Law No. 247-FZ must be established separately.

Court claim

Distinguish a claim challenging refusal to register from a claim for immediate payment. Assess jurisdiction and deadlines, and request the registration file if necessary. The ruling does not guarantee payment to every relative.
