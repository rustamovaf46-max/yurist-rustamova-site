Russian pension disputes: missing employment periods

How to challenge the Social Fund’s refusal to recognise employment periods for a Russian insurance pension.

Identify the disputed period

Obtain the written SFR decision and individual account statement. Compare employment dates, employer names, pension coefficients and special working conditions. Distinguish ordinary insurance periods from qualifying periods for early retirement.

Evidence under Federal Law No. 400-FZ

Depending on the date of registration in the pension insurance system, evidence may include employment records, contracts, appointment and dismissal orders, payroll records, archive certificates and individual account data. Explain changes of surname and discrepancies in dates.

When the employer has closed

A negative archive response does not prove that employment never occurred. Check successors, former company names and other archival collections. Witness evidence may be permitted under specific conditions, but cannot establish the special nature of work for early retirement.

Prepare the court claim

For each excluded period, identify the reason for refusal, supporting documents and effect on pension entitlement. Distinguish correction of individual account data, recognition of service, pension award and recalculation. Check procedural deadlines and request unavailable evidence through the court.

Why employment periods disappear

Errors in employment records, missing employer reports, liquidation, incomplete archive certificates, overlapping periods and foreign employment can require different legal solutions. Foreign employment requires examination of applicable international agreements. Non-contributory periods such as qualifying childcare or military service must be assessed separately under Russian law.

Build an evidence matrix

For each period record the start and end dates, employer, employment book entry, individual account entry, reason for refusal, supporting evidence and effect on eligibility or payment. Request payroll records, personnel orders, timesheets and archive certificates identifying their underlying records. For early retirement, establish the occupation, workplace, applicable occupational lists, actual duties and qualifying working conditions.

Administrative correction and pension application

Apply to the Social Fund of Russia (SFR) for correction of individual account data, attaching evidence for each period. A complaint to the superior SFR authority may resolve the dispute without litigation. Preserve the application, attachments and proof of submission. Where the pension authority identifies missing documents, submission within three months of its explanation can preserve the original pension application date under the applicable statutory conditions.

Supreme Court guidance of 26 June 2024

The Supreme Court's review of pension disputes discusses the importance of examining actual employment circumstances and the applicable historical rules. In a case involving an available employment book and information about another former employer, an oral refusal to seek records did not remove the need to establish relevant circumstances. This does not automatically establish every claimed period: identify the dates, employer, possible location of records and attempts to obtain them.

If records were lost without the employee's fault, testimony from at least two witnesses may establish certain periods subject to statutory conditions. Witnesses cannot establish the special nature of work for early retirement.

Adaptable court claim

To the competent district court. Claimant: [details]. Defendant: [competent territorial SFR body]. On [date], the claimant applied for [pension or correction]. Decision [number and date] excluded [exact periods] on the grounds of [reasons]. The attached documents establish [facts]. Explain the effect of each period on pension entitlement and the applicable provisions of Federal Law No. 400-FZ.

Requests: declare the challenged refusal unlawful in the relevant part; require inclusion of the specified periods in ordinary or special insurance service as appropriate; where the legal conditions are satisfied, require award or recalculation of the pension from the legally applicable date. Request the court to obtain identified records that cannot be obtained independently, explaining previous attempts and the relevance of each record. Attach the decision, individual account statement, employment documents, correspondence, calculation and procedural documents required for filing.

Why employment periods may be missing

Federal Law No. 400-FZ governs insurance periods, including qualifying non-contributory periods. Refusals may arise from illegible stamps, corrections, inconsistent names, missing employer reports, liquidation, incomplete archive certificates, overlapping periods, or foreign employment requiring examination of applicable international agreements. Obtain the written reasons and calculation, not merely an oral explanation.

Prepare a period-by-period record

Obtain your individual account statement through Gosuslugi or the Social Fund (SFR). Record exact dates, employment book entries, account data, supporting evidence, reasons for exclusion and the effect on pension entitlement. Check childcare, military service and other qualifying non-contributory periods where relevant. Even one month may affect early retirement eligibility.

Documents and witness evidence

Before registration in the individual accounting system, employment may be established through employer, public authority and archive records; after registration, individual account information is the primary source. Collect contracts, orders, payroll accounts, personnel cards, staffing schedules, timesheets, successor records and relevant tax information. Archive certificates should identify the applicant, dates, position, employer and underlying records. Civil registration documents can explain surname changes. Where records were lost through no fault of the employee, two or more former coworkers may establish certain periods under applicable rules, but not the special nature of work. Preserve written requests and negative archive responses.

Correction before litigation

Request specific corrections to the individual account through Gosuslugi or an SFR office. A complaint to the superior territorial SFR authority may resolve a refusal without litigation and does not remove the right to judicial protection. Preserve submissions, attachments and acknowledgements. When applying for a pension, supplying missing documents within three months of the SFR explanation can preserve the original application date. Monitor this deadline while awaiting archive responses.

Ordinary and special insurance periods

Early retirement may require evidence of full-time employment, occupation, production unit, working conditions and interruptions. Compare the position with applicable historical lists and rules. Obtain clarifying employer certificates, orders, staffing records, technical documents and individual accounting data. Do not assume proof of ordinary employment establishes special service.

Supreme Court guidance

The pension disputes review approved on 26 June 2024 addresses the need to establish actual employment circumstances and apply the rules in force during the relevant period. In the example discussed in the Russian article, an oral refusal to request records from a former employer did not remove the need to investigate relevant circumstances. This does not require automatic recognition of every claimed period. Identify the employer, dates and possible location of records; request a reasoned response. Under Article 57 of the Civil Procedure Code, request specific unavailable evidence, explaining its relevance and why it cannot be obtained independently.

Illustrative statement of claim

For an ordinary insurance-period dispute under the Civil Procedure Code, identify the district court, claimant and required identifiers, territorial SFR defendant, third parties if applicable, claim value where applicable and state duty or exemption. State the pension application date, refusal number and date, exact excluded employment dates, employer, position and stated reason. List supporting records, explain discrepancies and prior archive requests, and calculate the effect on pension rights.

Subject to the facts and applicable versions of Articles 11, 14 and 22 of Law No. 400-FZ and Articles 56 and 67 of the Civil Procedure Code, request: (1) recognition of the refusal as unlawful insofar as it excludes the specified period; (2) an order to include the specified period in insurance service; (3) only where entitlement and date are established, award or recalculation of the pension from the proper date; (4) documented litigation costs where legally justified.

Attach the refusal, account statement, employment book, name-change records, archive certificates and replies, calculation, proof of service on parties, state duty or exemption evidence, representative authority where applicable and other evidence. Sign and date the claim. This is not a universal template for early retirement, foreign employment or establishment of a legal fact. Check jurisdiction, fees and entitlement date. Article 22 does not permit choosing an arbitrary pension commencement date.

Frequently asked questions

Can records be corrected before retirement? Yes, apply to correct the individual account. Is an employment book sufficient? It is a principal document for periods before individual registration, but discrepancies or special conditions may require further evidence. Is one witness enough? The applicable rules require two or more witnesses in specified circumstances and do not establish special working conditions. Is an internal complaint mandatory? It may help resolve the dispute; the appropriate procedure depends on the refusal and relevant deadlines.
