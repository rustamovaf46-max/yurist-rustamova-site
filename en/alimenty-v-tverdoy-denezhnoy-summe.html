Fixed-amount child support in Russian courts

This page explains court proceedings under the law of the Russian Federation. A case-specific position requires review of the documents, the existing payment arrangement and the procedural deadlines.

When fixed support is available

Article 83 of the Russian Family Code permits a fixed monthly amount, or a combination of an income share and a fixed amount, where earnings are irregular, absent, paid in kind or in foreign currency, or where share-based recovery is difficult or materially prejudices a party. Formal employment alone does not rule out fixed support. The claimant must explain why the current method does not adequately protect the child and support that explanation with evidence.

How to substantiate the requested amount

The court seeks, as far as the circumstances allow, to preserve the child's former level of support. It considers both parents' financial and family circumstances, other maintenance duties, the child's needs and other relevant facts. Prepare a monthly calculation separating food, clothing, education, transport, medical care and other child-related costs. Seasonal purchases should be averaged over an appropriate period.

Shared household expenses must be allocated transparently. If housing, utilities or communications are included, explain the method used to identify the child's share. Receipts, contracts, bank statements and other records support the calculation; the total requested figure alone is not enough.

Subsistence minimum and indexation

Under Article 117, a fixed award is expressed by reference to the subsistence minimum for the relevant population group at the recipient's place of residence so that it can be indexed. It may be set as a multiple or a fraction. Moscow and Moscow Region use different regional figures; if no regional figure applies, the statutory federal fallback is used. The subsistence minimum is not an automatic award and does not replace proof of the child's needs and the parents' circumstances.

Documents and lawful evidence

  • birth and residence documents relevant to the child's maintenance;
  • an itemised expense calculation with available receipts, contracts and invoices;
  • evidence of the claimant's income and lawfully obtained information about the other parent;
  • bank statements, receipts and enforcement records showing actual payments;
  • any earlier court order, judgment, writ of execution or notarised maintenance agreement.

If a necessary record is held by an employer, bank or public authority and cannot be obtained independently, a focused court request may be appropriate. It should identify the document, explain its relevance and state why the party cannot obtain it. Unlawful access to another person's accounts or correspondence is not permissible.

Claim proceedings, jurisdiction and court orders

A fixed amount is determined in claim proceedings because the court must examine both the legal grounds and the amount; the simplified court-order procedure is not suitable for this demand. An initial claim for fixed child support is filed with the district court. Russian rules on alternative territorial jurisdiction may allow the claimant to file at their own residence or at the defendant's residence.

If paternity, a maintenance agreement or an existing award is also disputed, the remedies and jurisdiction must be checked together. Before filing, verify the current pleading requirements, service of copies on the participants and any applicable exemption from the state fee.

If support has already been awarded as an income share

The earlier judicial act remains relevant. Article 119 allows a party to seek a change where financial or family circumstances have changed, while a move to a fixed amount must also satisfy the conditions applicable to that method. Show what has changed: the pattern of actual payments, new documented expenses and why the existing arrangement should be revised. Indexation of an existing fixed award and changing the amount are different legal issues.

Illustrative calculation and wording of the claim

Suppose documented monthly child expenses total RUB 30,000. A claimant requests RUB 15,000 per month and explains the contribution they make themselves. This is an illustration, not a statutory equal division; the court considers both parents’ circumstances.

For an initial claim, request monthly child support in a fixed amount expressed as a fraction or multiple of the applicable child subsistence minimum at the recipient’s place of residence, subject to statutory indexation, from filing until majority. Specify the child, the monetary equivalent and evidence under Article 83 of the Russian Family Code. Modification of an existing award requires different wording and timing.

Do not assume concealed income without evidence. Where necessary, request specific documents through the court and distinguish litigation expenses from child support.

Frequently asked questions

Can I apply if the other parent has no official job?

Absence of earnings is one of the circumstances listed in Article 83, but the court still assesses both parties' position and the evidence needed to determine the amount.

Is it enough to name the desired monthly amount?

No. A calculation and supporting records are needed, together with an explanation of the child's previous level of support and the parents' resources.

Does the child's residence matter?

Yes. Residence and actual maintenance are relevant. If residence itself is disputed, it may require a separate claim.

Legal sources and limits of the service

Russian Family Code Articles 83, 117 and 119; Resolution of the Plenum of the Supreme Court of the Russian Federation No. 56 of 26 December 2017 on maintenance claims. The version of the law applicable on the filing date must be checked.
