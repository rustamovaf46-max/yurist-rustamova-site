Fixed-amount child support in Russian courts

Analytical adaptation of the Russian original. Individual circumstances require legal assessment.

When fixed support is available

Article 83 of the Russian Family Code permits fixed or combined share-based and fixed support where earnings are irregular, absent, paid in kind or foreign currency, or where share-based recovery is difficult or materially prejudices a party. Employment alone does not rule out fixed support.

Calculating the requested amount

Document the child's ordinary living standard and monthly needs: food, clothing, education, transport and medical costs. Explain the allocation of shared household expenses and average seasonal purchases. The court considers both parents' financial and family circumstances.

Indexation

Article 117 links fixed support to the applicable subsistence minimum for the relevant population group at the recipient's residence, subject to statutory federal fallback rules. Moscow and Moscow Region have different figures. The minimum is not an automatic award.

Evidence and procedure

Collect birth and residence documents, expense records, income evidence, payment history and existing enforcement documents. If records cannot be obtained independently, request specific evidence through the court. Fixed support requires claim proceedings rather than a summary court order; verify jurisdiction and any existing award.
