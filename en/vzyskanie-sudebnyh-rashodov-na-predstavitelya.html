Recovering lawyers’ and other court costs in Russian civil proceedings

Русский · English · 中文 · العربية

Winning a civil case does not automatically mean recovering every ruble spent on a lawyer, expert reports or postage. Recovery generally requires evidence of the services, their connection to the proceedings, actual expenditure or a legally recognised discharge of the payment obligation, and a reasonable amount. The deadline must be checked against the actual sequence of court decisions.

What qualifies as litigation costs

Chapter 7 of the Russian Code of Civil Procedure (CPC/GPK RF) distinguishes state court fees from litigation-related expenses. Article 94 includes legal representation, experts, specialists, witnesses, relevant travel, accommodation and postage, and certain other necessary expenses accepted by the court. Merely spending money during a dispute does not make every payment recoverable. The applicant must explain why it was needed for the specific proceedings.

Evidence of legal fees

A strong application includes the engagement agreement and scope of work, invoices or completion statements where available, and proof of payment such as bank statements, payment orders, receipts or valid acknowledgements. Provide copies of submissions prepared by the lawyer and records of hearing attendance. If one payment covers several lawsuits, allocate it to the relevant case with a transparent calculation. For other expenses, attach postal receipts, expert-fee invoices and evidence of necessity.

Why courts may reduce the claimed amount

Article 100 CPC permits the successful party to recover representation costs only within reasonable limits. Supreme Court Plenum Resolution No. 1 of 21 January 2016 explains that the court can consider complexity, the amount at stake, work actually performed, number of hearings, drafting time, duration and prices of comparable services. No legal formula guarantees full recovery or an automatic 50% reduction. If the opponent challenges excessive fees, respond with case-specific evidence rather than the contract price alone.

When the claim succeeded only in part

Article 98 CPC generally allocates costs proportionally where claims are partially successful, with qualifications for certain types of claims. Mixed monetary and non-monetary demands, several independent issues or appeals can require separate treatment. A simple ratio applied to all invoices may therefore be incorrect. Review which expenses related to the successful portions of the case and whether earlier orders have already dealt with them.

Where and when to apply

Under Article 103.1 CPC, unresolved costs are claimed in the court that heard the case at first instance. The general time limit is three months from the date on which the last judicial act concluding the proceedings entered into force. This can include appellate or cassation-stage costs, where their allocation remains unresolved. The relevant 'last act' and entry-into-force date depend on the litigation history. A court may restore a missed deadline for a valid reason, but that is not a substitute for timely filing.

Illustrative deadline calculation

If the last appellate determination concluding the proceedings was issued and took effect on 15 September, an uncomplicated three-month period would ordinarily end on 15 December. If the final day is a non-working day, Article 108 CPC may move it to the next working day. Receiving a paper copy later does not by itself reset the clock. An intention to lodge cassation proceedings also should not lead to an unexamined delay; check what proceedings actually ended and which expenses remain outstanding.

How to support a reasonable amount

Create a chronology listing each legal task, its date, the written submission or hearing, and the agreed cost. Match the documents to the contract and proof of payment. Explain why the number of sessions, technical evidence, complexity and appeal work were necessary. Where market comparisons are used, compare genuinely similar services and regions, not the cheapest unrelated listing. Separate proof of actual expense from proof that the amount is reasonable.

What a costs application must state

Identify the first-instance court, case number, parties, final court acts, the procedural result and exactly which costs were incurred. List by category the person who paid, the amount, date and link to a particular action. Cite the applicable CPC provisions and supply the documents proving payment, services and necessity. Ask for a specific sum and itemise other expenses separately. Do not claim costs already awarded without identifying a lawful basis for reopening or correcting that allocation.

Sample application to recover representation costs

TO [first-instance court], civil case No. [number]. APPLICANT: [identity, address, party status]. OTHER PARTY: [identity, address]. APPLICATION FOR RECOVERY OF LITIGATION COSTS. [Court] heard [party v. party] concerning [claim]. Judgment dated [date], as affected by [appellate/cassation act if applicable], resolved the dispute [result]. Under the engagement agreement dated [date], the applicant retained [lawyer] to perform [list of submissions and hearings] in this specific case and incurred RUB [amount], evidenced by [payment and service documents]. These expenses were necessary and reasonable because [specific explanation]. Their allocation has not previously been determined. Under CPC Articles 94, 98, 100 and 103.1 and Plenum Resolution No. 1 of 21 January 2016, I REQUEST: (1) recovery from [other party] of RUB [amount] for legal representation; (2) recovery of other specifically evidenced expenses of RUB [amount] where legally appropriate. ATTACHMENTS: engagement agreement, service records, evidence of expenditure or lawful discharge, detailed calculations, relevant judgments, proof of sending the application to parties and authority documents where required. [Date, signature]. Adapt the example to the actual case.

A significant change: Constitutional Court ruling No. 60-P, 20 October 2026

The Russian Constitutional Court held that when the successful litigant assigns to its representative the right to recover representation costs from the losing party as payment for legal services, the costs must be considered incurred by virtue of the assignment in the circumstances addressed by the ruling. Such a transfer ordinarily discharges the client's obligation to remunerate the representative. This does not grant automatic recovery of the agreed sum: the court still checks genuine services, the relationship to the case, entitlement to claim and reasonableness under Articles 98 and 100 CPC. Keep the legal-service contract, assignment agreement, records of performed work and documentation of the discharged obligation.

Potentially recoverable pre-litigation and later costs

Plenum Resolution No. 1 recognises that expenses needed to comply with a legally obligatory pre-trial procedure may in the relevant circumstances form litigation costs. In addition, costs genuinely incurred after the final judgment for work connected with the dispute may be considered under the applicable procedural route. Their necessity and connection to the lawsuit must still be documented. Separate the rules for civil, commercial (APK) and administrative (KAS) proceedings.

Common errors and FAQs

A contract alone is not always enough: document the service and payment or lawful settlement mechanism. A lawyer charging RUB 50,000 does not guarantee a RUB 50,000 award. Include expert and postal costs only where supported and necessary. If proceedings passed through multiple instances, trace the final acts and determine which costs have been assessed. Do not confuse the three-month application deadline with the period for appealing the substantive judgment.
