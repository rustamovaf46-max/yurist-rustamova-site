Family court disputes in Moscow

Fatima Rustamova provides representation in Russian family disputes in Moscow and the Moscow Region. The legal position depends on the parties' circumstances, the available evidence, procedural stage and the interests of children where relevant. Assistance may include drafting claims, objections, motions, reviewing evidence and attending hearings.

Child residence and contact arrangements

Disputes about where a child lives and how a parent maintains contact require consideration of the child's interests and relevant circumstances. Evidence may concern living conditions, parental involvement, the child's needs and other facts. A court may involve guardianship authorities where required by law. No particular outcome can be promised.

Child maintenance and enforcement

Maintenance claims may involve a share of earnings, a fixed amount or a combination where statutory conditions are met. The form and amount depend on income information, the child's circumstances and the applicable Family Code provisions. Arrears, enforcement measures and statutory penalties require a separate examination of the payment history and enforcement records.

Establishing or challenging paternity

Paternity proceedings can involve civil-status records, witness and documentary evidence and genetic examination. A private DNA result does not automatically replace evidence evaluated under court procedure. The court determines the admissibility and weight of the material and whether a forensic examination is necessary.

Division of marital assets and debts

Property disputes require identifying the assets, acquisition dates, title documents, possible personal-property exceptions, valuations and liabilities. A court does not necessarily divide every debt simply because the parties were married; the legal basis and use of borrowed funds matter. Limitation issues must be assessed from the relevant facts, not by assuming one universal date.

Preparation and court procedure

Collect marriage or divorce records, children's birth certificates where relevant, income documents, property and debt records, correspondence, court documents and existing enforcement materials. Before filing, review jurisdiction, claims, evidence and deadlines. If a case is already pending, provide the court, case number and nearest hearing date.

Fees and realistic expectations

Court representation starts at RUB 50,000 per instance. The final fee and possible additional expenses, including expert examinations, are agreed before engagement. No lawyer can guarantee a judgment. The court evaluates the evidence and applies Russian law to the individual case.

First contact and confidentiality

Briefly describe the dispute and the procedural stage, without sending children's personal information or complete confidential documents through public channels. Agree a suitable method for sharing sensitive records before transmission.
