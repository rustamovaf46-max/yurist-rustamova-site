Child contact arrangements after divorce in Russia

Analytical adaptation of the Russian original; individual cases require separate assessment.

Agreement and court proceedings

Article 66 of the Russian Family Code protects the separately residing parent's right to communicate with the child and participate in upbringing and education. Parents may agree in writing; otherwise the district court determines arrangements with mandatory guardianship authority participation.

An enforceable schedule

Specify days, start and end times, handover location, transport, holidays, overnight stays, video calls and arrangements for illness. Avoid vague terms requiring fresh agreement for every meeting.

Best interests and evidence

Under Supreme Court Plenum Resolution No. 10 of 27 May 1998, paragraph 8, courts assess age, health, routine, attachment, travel distance and safety. Article 57 requires consideration of the views of children aged ten or older unless contrary to their interests. Collect lawful correspondence, school schedules, housing evidence and records of caregiving.

Interim contact and restrictions

Article 66(2) permits interim arrangements pending the final judgment, with guardianship authority participation. Contact may be restricted where it harms the child's physical or mental health or moral development. A proposed schedule should address documented risks rather than unsupported allegations.
