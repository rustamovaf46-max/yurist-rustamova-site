Interim measures in a Russian land dispute: how to justify a restriction on registration

28.11.2026

Why interim relief matters

When ownership, boundaries or a cadastral register error are contested, a claimant may worry that the property will be sold or mortgaged before judgment. A general concern is not enough. The court needs evidence of a concrete risk to enforcement and must assess whether the requested measure is proportionate. This article concerns Russian law, not the law of another country.

Legal grounds and jurisdiction

Articles 139–144 of the Russian Civil Procedure Code (GPK RF) govern interim measures in civil proceedings; Articles 90–97 of the Arbitrazh Procedure Code (APK RF) apply in commercial court proceedings. The court and procedure depend on the parties and nature of the claim. An interim order neither determines the merits nor proves ownership.

Evidence of the risk

Connect the proposed restriction to the precise property and the relief claimed. Potential evidence includes a recent extract from the Unified State Register of Real Estate (EGRN), correspondence about an intended sale or mortgage, a published sale advertisement, draft transaction documents and other verifiable facts. An assertion that the defendant might sell the land, without more, may not suffice.

Drafting the application

Identify the court, parties, case number if assigned, cadastral number and property address. State the substantive claim, the exact interim measure sought, the facts suggesting that enforcement could otherwise become difficult or impossible, and attach the supporting documents. Avoid a blanket restraint on all assets where the dispute concerns one specific parcel.

Proportionality and third parties

The measure should protect the potential judgment without unnecessarily prejudicing the opposing party or others. A ban on registration, an attachment of property and a prohibition on specified transactions are not interchangeable. An anticipated mortgage application does not automatically establish an enforceability risk; explain its actual connection to the case.

After the court's order

Check the cadastral identifier, the wording of the order, the required transmission for execution and whether the restriction is reflected in EGRN. An interim measure may be varied or lifted under the applicable procedural rules. In some circumstances unjustified interim relief can lead to a claim for losses.

Practical conclusion

A persuasive application presents a traceable chain: the claim, the specific risk, the likely impact on enforcement and the necessity of a narrowly drawn measure. Before filing, confirm the current statutory wording, jurisdiction and suitability of the requested relief to the facts.

This is general information about Russian law, not advice about a specific dispute.
