Preparing for your first enquiry

State which court is hearing the dispute, the current stage, the next hearing date or appeal deadline, the result you seek and which documents you have. A brief initial description is sufficient. Do not send passport details, children's information or complete confidential case files through open channels before agreeing a secure method of transfer.

Frequently asked questions

Can I contact you after a hearing has been scheduled? Yes, but preparation and participation depend on the hearing date, available case materials and necessary procedural steps. Mention the nearest deadline.

Can you assist with an appeal or cassation complaint? The options are assessed after examining the decisions, applicable deadlines and grounds for review.

Remote work and costs

Document review and procedural preparation can be discussed remotely. Court attendance and authority to act are agreed separately. State duties, expert examinations, notarial services and other third-party costs may be additional.
