Division of marital property after divorce in Russia: limitation period

Analytical adaptation of the Russian original; individual cases require separate assessment.

The three-year rule

Article 38(7) of the Russian Family Code establishes a three-year limitation period for former spouses' property division claims. Under paragraph 19 of Supreme Court Plenum Resolution No. 15 of 5 November 1998 and Article 200 of the Civil Code, the relevant date is when the claimant knew or should have known of the infringement, not automatically the divorce date.

Evidence of infringement

Denial of ownership rights, exclusion from jointly used property or disposal contrary to the other spouse's rights may indicate infringement. Establish when the claimant learned or should have learned of it. Later correspondence does not erase earlier evidence of a dispute.

Illustrative example

If spouses divorced in 2021 but retained agreed access to their apartment until one changed the locks and denied the other's rights in 2026, the court must examine the 2026 events and any earlier infringement. This hypothetical example does not guarantee an outcome.

Preparing the claim

Build a chronology for each asset, including purchase documents, funding, registry extracts, marriage records, agreements, correspondence and prior proceedings. Registration in one spouse's name does not alone determine the property regime. Do not substitute an access claim for division merely to circumvent limitation.
