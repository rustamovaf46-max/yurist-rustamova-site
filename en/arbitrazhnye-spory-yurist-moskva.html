Commercial litigation lawyer in Moscow | Russian arbitrazh courts

Russian state arbitrazh courts are commercial courts, not private international arbitration tribunals. This is an English adaptation; the Russian original contains further detail.

Commercial disputes before Russian courts

Fatima Rustamova provides court representation in business disputes in Moscow and the Moscow Region. The proper forum, claim and procedural steps depend on the parties' status, the contract, the nature of the dispute and applicable Russian law. State arbitrazh courts generally handle commercial and other economic disputes within their statutory jurisdiction.

Debt recovery and contract performance

Disputes may arise from supply, services, construction and other commercial agreements. A claim requires examining the contract, acceptance documents, invoices, correspondence, payment records and whether obligations were properly performed. Penalties, interest and losses require a separate legal and evidentiary basis.

Pre-action procedure and deadlines

Many Russian commercial claims require compliance with a mandatory pre-action claim procedure, subject to statutory exceptions. The form and waiting period depend on the claim and applicable rules. Filing too early or without proof of compliance can cause procedural difficulties. Limitation periods and appeal deadlines must be checked individually.

Evidence and electronic correspondence

Documents, electronic communications, accounting records and evidence of delivery or acceptance may be relevant. A screenshot alone may be insufficient if authorship, integrity or context is disputed. The court evaluates admissibility, reliability and the evidence as a whole under the Commercial Procedure Code.

Interim measures and enforcement

Where legal grounds are present, a party may request interim measures to protect effective enforcement of a future judgment. Such measures are not automatic; the applicant must justify the request under the procedural rules. Enforcement after judgment may require additional procedural steps.

Challenging judgments and procedural errors

Appeal and cassation involve different grounds, deadlines and review limits. A complaint should identify material errors of law or procedure, cite the record and explain how the errors affected the decision. A mere disagreement with the court's assessment is not necessarily sufficient for cassation.

Costs and scope of representation

Services may include case assessment, drafting a claim or response, pre-action correspondence, procedural motions, hearing attendance and appeal analysis. Representation starts at RUB 50,000 per court instance. State fees, expert examinations and other external expenses may be additional. The final scope and fee are agreed in advance; no outcome is guaranteed.

Initial enquiry

Provide the contract type, amount in dispute, whether a pre-action claim was sent, the court and case number if proceedings are pending, and the nearest deadline. Do not send confidential commercial information through public channels before agreeing a secure method.
